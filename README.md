# 📱 AURA - AI-Powered Personal Styling App

> *"Not just a shopping app, but a style assistant. A personalized fashion experience powered by artificial intelligence."*

AURA is an AI-powered Super-App designed to solve "Choice Overload" and "Decision Fatigue" in modern fashion e-commerce. By learning the user's style and proactively offering outfit suggestions, AURA transforms shopping from a chore into a seamless experience.

## 🔍 The Challenge: Why AURA?
When analyzing dominant players like Zara and ASOS, we identified critical UX gaps:
1. **Decision Fatigue:** Users like individual items but abandon carts because they don't know how to style them.
2. **Navigation Maze:** Crucial categories are often hidden behind Hamburger Menus.
3. **Visual Noise:** Heavy focus on editorial aesthetics compromises basic readability and accessibility.

## 📊 User Research & Insights
We conducted detailed surveys (20 participants) and Guerrilla Testing (5 participants) to drive our design decisions:
* **80%** of users struggle with creating outfits. -> *Solution: AI Stylist Integration.*
* **65%** face size/fit uncertainty. -> *Solution: AI-based size recommendations.*
* **60%** prefer Dark Mode for evening shopping. -> *Solution: Native Dark Mode support (WCAG 2.1 compliance).*

## 💡 Key Design Solutions
* **AI Stylist (Google Gemini API):** Recommends cross-sell items (e.g., matching shoes for a jacket) to eliminate decision fatigue and increase basket value.
* **Frictionless Checkout:** Reduced a typical 5+ step checkout into a seamless Bottom Sheet interaction without leaving the cart screen.
* **Thumb-Friendly Navigation:** Replaced the traditional hamburger menu with a highly accessible Bottom Nav bar.
* **Accessibility First (A11Y):** Integrated Dynamic Large Text, Reduce Motion options, and a high-contrast Dark Theme.
